My Journey

01 — The Beginning

My name is Aderibigbe Oluwafikunayomi Adedamola, known as Damola thestrategist. I'm a technology enthusiast, creative thinker, and someone passionate about turning ideas into reality.

02 — Entering the Digital World

My journey into the digital world began in 2023, when I joined a 7-day online graphic design class on WhatsApp. I had no prior knowledge of graphic design, but that experience became the starting point of something much bigger. After the class, I continued learning, practicing, and building myself from the ground up. Over time, I developed my skills, explored different areas of creativity, and discovered my passion for using digital skills to bring ideas to life.

In 2025, I expanded my journey into affiliate marketing, which introduced me to the world of digital marketing, online business, and digital products. This experience helped me understand how creativity, technology, and strategy can come together to create value in the digital space.

03 — University & Computer Engineering

That same year, I reached another major milestone when I gained admission into Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), Ogbomoso, Oyo State, Nigeria, to study Computer Engineering.

My university experience continues to strengthen my technical foundation while giving me opportunities to develop my creative and digital skills.

04 — Building Something of My Own

As I continued growing, I began building something of my own. I developed the identity Damola thestrategist, a name that represents my approach to creativity, technology, strategy, and continuous learning.

Alongside this personal brand, I’m also building Digital HQ, a long-term brand through which I aim to create and deliver digital and technology-driven solutions.

05 — The Next Level

In 2026, while actively searching for opportunities to expand my technical knowledge, I discovered the TS Academy Scholarship sponsored by Asherkine. I applied to study DevOps Engineering and was fortunate to be awarded the scholarship.

This opportunity is helping me deepen my understanding of software engineering and DevOps while taking me closer to my long-term career goal of becoming a DevOps Engineer.

06 — The Journey Continues

From graphic design to digital marketing, from university to software and DevOps, every stage has been building on the one before it.

And this is only the beginning.

My goal is to keep learning, keep building, and grow Damola the Strategist into a recognizable name in the creator and technology space, while building Digital HQ into a lasting brand that represents innovation, digital solutions, and meaningful ideas brought to life.

The journey continues.
